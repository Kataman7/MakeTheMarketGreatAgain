# Rules

Welcome to **Make The Market Great Again**.

## Materials

- **21 Company cards** (split across Tech, Industry, Media, Health and 5 geographic zones).
- **18 Tweet cards** (with bonuses, penalties and shields).
- **Unlimited money** for each player to bid at auctions.

## Goal of the game

Accumulate the most **victory points (VP)** with your companies while surviving the crises... and above all **do not be the player who spent the most money** at the end of the game.

## Setup

- Separate the cards into two distinct decks: the **Companies** deck and the **Tweets** deck.
- Shuffle each deck face down.

## Turn sequence

Each turn, draw and play a **Company** card.

### Secret auction

1. Each player secretly prepares the amount of their choice in their hand.
2. **Reveal**: everyone shows their bid at the same time. The highest bidder pays the bank and takes the Company into their portfolio.
3. **Warning**: if two players make exactly the same maximum bid, nobody wins the card, but all the money bid is lost.

### The Tweets

At regular intervals (for example every 2 companies bought, or at your own pace), draw and read a Trump Tweet out loud. Place it in the center of the table: it will modify the value of the cards at the end of the game.

## End of game and scoring

The game stops instantly as soon as the last Company card in the deck has been sold. Scoring happens in two strict steps:

### 1. The Elimination Trap (Q.E. Trap)

All players publicly announce the total amount of money they spent from the start to the end of the game. The player who spent the most money is **immediately eliminated by bankruptcy**, regardless of their points.

### 2. Point Calculation (for the survivors)

- Add up the base VP of your companies (Tech = 4, Industry = 3, Media = 2, Health = 1).
- Then apply the bonuses and penalties of all the Tweets placed on the table.
- Don't forget to check your cards: if you own the required sectors or countries, your **Shields cancel the penalties**.

The survivor with the most VP wins. **A player with 0 VP at the end is also eliminated: you need a positive number of VP to win.**
