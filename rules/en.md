# Rules

Welcome to **Make The Market Great Again**.

## How to play

1. Type a bid amount (ex: `100`, `50k`, `2.5M`, `-1B`).
2. Press **Bid** to set a pending bid.
3. Press **Validate** to confirm and add it to the total.
4. Press **Cancel** to discard the pending bid.

## Values

- Bids can be positive or negative.
- `0` is allowed.
- Large numbers are automatically abbreviated (`K`, `M`, `B`, `T`, ...).

## End of game

Press **End Game** to view the history of validated bids and the total, then the game resets automatically.
