# GAME RULES

### 1. GOAL OF THE GAME

Play as the Finance Minister of a superpower (Japan, Europe, Korea, USA, or China) and build the most powerful **stock portfolio**. Accumulate as many Victory Points (VP) as you can while surviving crises... **without triggering a fatal hyperinflation.**

### 2. MATERIALS

* **5 Country cards** (Japan, Europe, Korea, USA, China).
* **21 Stock cards** (with a sector, a country of origin, and a VP value).
* **18 Tweet cards** (bonuses, penalties, and shields).
* *Note:* To manage money and track spending, **scan the QR code** to use the dedicated website (then press the "PLAY" button).

### 3. SETUP

1. Each player chooses or draws a **Country card** and places it in front of them.
2. Separate the rest of the game into two decks: the **Stocks deck** and the **Tweets deck**. Shuffle them and place them face down in the center.
3. Each player takes out their phone, **scans the QR code** to access the money printer website and prepare to print currency.
4. **Visibility:** You must place and display all your acquired Stock cards face up on the table for everyone to see. However, you may hide the total amount of money you have spent.

### 4. ANATOMY OF A STOCK

Each Stock card has 3 key attributes:

1. **Base Victory Points (VP):** Determined by its sector (Tech = **4 VP** | Industry = **3 VP** | Media = **2 VP** | Health = **1 VP**).
2. **Sector:** Tech, Industry, Media, or Health.
3. **Country of origin:** China, Europe, Japan, or Korea.

### 5. TURN SEQUENCE

Each turn, a new **company stock enters the market.**

#### Step A: The Stock Auction (via the website)

1. Reveal the top Stock card from the deck.
2. **Free bidding:** Use your unlimited access to the digital money printer on the website to bid any amount you choose.
3. **Reveal:** Everyone reveals their bid at the same time. The highest bidder takes the stock and adds it face up to their portfolio on the table.
* **The Equality Trap:** If two ministers make exactly the same maximum bid, the stock is planted (nobody gets it) and all the money printed for that auction is permanently burned.

#### Step B: Trump's Tweets

* At regular intervals, draw and read a **Trump Tweet** out loud at the center of the table. It permanently changes the market value of sectors until the end of the game.

### 6. SPECIAL RULES: MONOPOLIES

If you hold a large number of cards of the same type at the end of the game, you benefit from major advantages:

* **Monopoly or Duopoly (majority of cards):** The player who owns the most cards (or tied for duopoly) in a domain is protected: **negative effects from Tweet cards do not apply** to them.
* **Strict Media Monopoly:** If you hold an absolute monopoly on Media, when crises are applied, **draw 2 Tweet cards, choose which one applies to the game, and return the other to the bottom of the deck.**

### 7. END OF GAME AND SCORING

The game ends instantly as soon as the last Stock card in the deck is sold. The market summit concludes in two steps:

#### Step 1: The Bankruptcy Trap (Sovereign Debt)

* Each player reveals the **total cumulative amount of all money they printed and injected** (using the data recorded on the website) from the start of the game.
* **The minister who spent the most money is immediately removed (eliminated)** for bankruptcy, regardless of their points.

#### Step 2: Point Calculation (For the survivors)

1. **Base market value:** Add up the VP of your stocks (Tech = 4, Industry = 3, Media = 2, Health = 1).
2. **Tweet effects:** Apply the bonuses/penalties of active Tweets on the market (unless your monopolies protect you).
3. **Geographic shields:** If your stocks possess the required sectors or zones, your shields cancel penalties.
4. **Victory:** The surviving minister with **the most VP** (and a strictly positive score above 0) wins the game.
